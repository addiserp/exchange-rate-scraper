What we implement

Two core packages. One Odoo system underneath.

Every engagement starts from your operation's real shape — heavy on people, or heavy on items — then we add the modules you actually need on top.

Package 01

HRM + Attendance + Payroll

For factories, flower farms, and any employer running large manpower across shifts, sites or seasonal harvests. This is the package behind our Braam Flowers, Ethiopian Postal Service, Coffee Export and Desert Locust Control implementations.

  • Employee master data, contracts and job positions
  • Biometric / fingerprint attendance devices, multi-site
  • Shift rosters and overtime capture
  • Automated payroll runs with Ethiopian deduction rules
  • Leave requests, approvals and recruitment pipeline

Package 02

Sales + Purchase + Inventory

For wholesalers, retailers and distributors handling large item counts across one or more stores. This is the package behind our Hast Enterprise, EthioBASE Business Group and Ethiopian Commodities implementations.

  • Multi-store point of sale and sales order flow
  • Purchase requests, vendor bills and approval chains
  • Real-time stock levels across warehouses
  • Barcode scanning and low-stock alerts
  • Supply chain and light manufacturing (MRP) add-on
Add-on for either package

Chapa & Telebirr payment integration

Collections and transfers post directly against open invoices inside Odoo — cashiers and finance teams see the same numbers.

Chapa

Online checkout & invoicing

Customers pay invoices or online orders through Chapa; payment status updates automatically in Odoo accounting.

Telebirr

Mobile money collection

Field agents and cashiers record Telebirr payments at the point of sale, reconciled against the day's sales orders.

Bank

Transfer reconciliation

Bank transfer receipts matched to customer invoices, cutting manual end-of-day reconciliation.
