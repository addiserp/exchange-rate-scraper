Who we are

An Addis Ababa team focused only on Odoo ERP

AddisERP Solution PLC is an information technology firm that supports business automation for Ethiopian companies, with our practice concentrated entirely on Odoo ERP implementation.

Company profile

Systems built for how Ethiopian businesses operate

We are a systems solutions provider with a reputation for understanding client needs and delivering on them. Operating from Addis Ababa, we deliver consistent Odoo ERP service across the capital and beyond.

Our focus stays on early project entry and full integration — combined with tracking Odoo's evolving releases and IT trends — so clients get results that hold up after go-live, not just at demo time.

With a professional bench of programmers, system analysts and trainers, we offer end-to-end consulting for business automation, concentrating on developing and customizing web-based ERP software, principally on the Odoo platform.
