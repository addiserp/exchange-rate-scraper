Hast Enterprise: retiring a custom ERP for Odoo 18 Community

Nine years running on a system AddisERP built and maintained by hand — replaced with a standard, actively developed platform, timed to go live on the first day of the new Ethiopian business year.

The challenge

A capable system that had become a one-off to maintain

AddisERP originally built and supported a locally-developed ERP system for Hast Enterprise starting in 2017, covering their sales, purchase and inventory operations. It did the job for years — but a fully custom system means every improvement, integration and fix depends on a single development team rather than a wider platform ecosystem.

As Hast Enterprise's needs evolved, both the client and AddisERP saw the case for moving onto a standard platform instead of continuing to extend the custom build.
